Igatpuri Tour

Hills, Waterfall & Dams

Get drivers who act as a guide

 

Igatpuri is a small hill town in the Western Ghats, located in Nashik district of Maharashtra. It’s best known for its scenic beauty, cool climate, and peaceful vibe especially popular with travelers from Mumbai and Pune looking for a quick nature getaway.

Top Attractions

 

 

🌄 Kasara Ghat – Scenic mountain pass with breathtaking valley views, especially during monsoon.

 

🕉️ Kapaleshwar Cave – A peaceful cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.

 

🙏Ghatandevi Mata Mandir – Surrounded by hills, perfect for a spiritual and nature-filled visit.

 

🚪Myanmar Gate – A grand entrance near Vipassana, inspired by Burmese architecture.

 

💧Ashoka Waterfall Vihigaon – A popular waterfall known for adventure activities like rappelling.

 

🌊 Bhavali Dam – Calm and scenic dam ideal for relaxing and photography.

 

🏰 Tringalwadi Fort – A trekking spot with panoramic views from the top.

 

⛰️ Camel Valley – Known for dramatic cliffs and seasonal waterfalls.

 

🌿 Bhatsa River Valley – A lush green valley with misty landscapes and river views.

 

🧘 Vipassana International Academy – World-famous meditation center offering a peaceful environment.
